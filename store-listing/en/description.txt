NAME
Alien Helper

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A lightweight Alienware Command Center alternative for compatible Dell Alienware laptops: power modes, fans, lighting and monitoring in one small window.

DESCRIPTION
Alien Helper is a compact control app for Dell Alienware laptops. One window and a tray icon, with no services or background agents of its own.

The app is intended only for compatible Dell Alienware laptops. Tested on the Alienware 16 Area-51 (AA16250). It should work on Alienware laptops with Alienware Command Center from 2020 on (m15/m17 R3–R5, x14/x15/x17, m16/m18, Alienware 16/18 Area-51, 16 Aurora / 16X Aurora). On other computers power modes and fan control are not available.

Power modes
• Balanced, Balanced Performance, Cool, Quiet, Performance, Alien Mode — the list comes from the laptop's firmware.
• Custom mode: CPU and GPU fans follow their own temperature → speed curves with smooth ramping.
• Built-in curve profiles (Quiet, Balanced, Performance, Maximum cooling) and your own.
• Overclock in Custom mode: a cautious NVIDIA GPU overclock that restores the previous clocks.
• Hotkeys to switch modes, with an on-screen hint.

Fans
• Live speed of every fan.
• Fan boost with one slider, or separately for CPU and GPU.

Monitoring
• CPU, GPU, VRAM, RAM, SSD, network and the laptop's power draw.
• Temperature of every CPU core and of the video memory (where the sensors are available).
• Charts for the last 5 minutes and an in-game overlay with FPS.

Lighting
• Keyboard and chassis color and brightness.
• Per-key lighting editor with templates and animations (fire, ocean, rainbow).

More
• Dark, light and strict themes, and a numbers-only mode.
• 6 languages: English, Russian, French, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese.
• Starts with Windows.

Controlling power modes and fans requires administrator rights: the laptop firmware exposes them to administrators only.
